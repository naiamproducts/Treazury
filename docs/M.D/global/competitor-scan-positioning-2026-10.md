# Treazury — competitive scan + external positioning stress-test (2026-10)

Scope: M.D / global. Decision this unlocks: (a) confirm or correct the
founder's competitor roster; (b) ADOPT/ADAPT/REJECT the positioning statement
an external AI proposed. Verdict vocabulary: market/positioning →
`VALIDATED / PROMISING / UNVALIDATED / REJECTED`; factual findings →
`CONFIRMED / SUSPICIOUS / INFORMATIONAL`. Threat verdicts per competitor are
`ALTA / MEDIA / BAJA` for the validated beachhead (MX/US merchanting of goods,
tickets $15K–200K, EN-only V1).

## Headline finding (the one that matters most)

**Nobody found in this scan does exactly what Treazury does**: a
**non-custodial** flow (buyer pays the seller's wallet directly, platform
never holds funds) where the platform **verifies the USDC payment on-chain
and releases the generated commercial document pack** (invoice + packing
list) as the conditional act, for triangular-trade/merchanting ICP. Three
searches aimed at the exact combination returned zero results (sources above:
"trade documents"+"stablecoin" settlement; "merchanting"/"triangular trade"
+USDC; both INFORMATIONAL — absence of evidence, not proof of absence).

The conditional-settlement space splits into two camps, and neither is ours:

1. **Custodial escrow that gates MONEY on documents** — Tazapay (escrow
 agreement includes "Documents to be verified before release",
 support.tazapay.com, CONFIRMED), Escrow.com, Vaultion (smart-contract lock,
 release tied to delivery/inspection/bill of lading, CONFIRMED from their
 page). They hold/lock value first; documents are the unlock key.
2. **Non-custodial payment/invoicing with no document layer** — Offbank
 ("non-custodial USDC settlement", CONFIRMED from their page), Request
 Network (invoice + on-chain payment link), Linedger/GramPayBot-class
 invoicing. Payment moves; no commercial doc pack, no merchanting ICP.

Treazury inverts both: **money moves first (client→client, direct), then we
release DOCUMENTS.** That inversion is the white space — CONFIRMED as "no
match found in this scan", INFORMATIONAL as a market-wide claim (re-check
before pitching).
